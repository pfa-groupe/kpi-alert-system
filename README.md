# KPI Alert System
### Predictive KPI Monitoring & Early Warning System

## Overview

**KPI Alert System** is a predictive monitoring project designed to identify potential underperformance in Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) before it becomes a critical issue.

Instead of relying only on historical reporting or reacting after a KPI falls below its target, the system aims to analyze KPI trends, detect potential deviations, and generate early warnings to support proactive decision-making.

The goal is to help organizations move from reactive performance monitoring to a more predictive and data-driven approach.

## Objectives

- Monitor KPI performance over time.
- Analyze historical KPI trends and variations.
- Identify indicators showing signs of potential underperformance.
- Apply predictive approaches to anticipate performance deviations.
- Generate alerts to help users identify KPIs requiring attention.
- Support faster and better-informed decision-making.

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Preparation** – Collect, organize, and prepare KPI-related data.
2. **Feature Engineering** – Extract relevant features from historical KPI observations.
3. **Time Series Analysis** – Analyze temporal patterns, trends, and variations.
4. **Predictive Modeling** – Use predictive approaches to estimate future KPI behavior.
5. **Alert Generation** – Identify potential underperformance and trigger alerts based on defined conditions.
